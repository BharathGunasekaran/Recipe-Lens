# 🥘 Food Recipe Generator

A smart and intuitive application that generates recipe suggestions based on the input food or ingredient image. Simply upload an image, and get a list of delicious recipes you can make with the detected items!

## 📌 Features

- 📷 Image-based ingredient recognition
- 🍽️ Recipe suggestions based on detected ingredients
- 📝 User-friendly interface
- 🙌 Useful for reducing food waste and encouraging home cooking

## 🎯 Use Case

Ever wondered what to cook with the leftover items in your kitchen? Just take a picture of them and let the Food Recipe Generator suggest creative and tasty recipes!

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Upload** an image of food or ingredients.
2. The app **analyzes** the image and detects the ingredients.
3. It then **suggests** relevant recipes you can make with those ingredients.
